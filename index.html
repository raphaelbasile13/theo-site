Questions, answered

Anything else? Write to us at appsvokko@gmail.com

Is Theo free?

Your first verdict is free. After that it’s Theo Pro: yearly, with a 7-day free trial if you’re new to it, or monthly. You’ll see the exact price in the app before you pay anything.

Are my photos stored?

No. Your photo goes to Theo to be read and is never stored on our servers. The looks you save stay on your phone.

Will Theo comment on my body?

Never. Not your body, weight, skin or age. He talks about the look: the clothes, how they work together and whether they fit the occasion. Face and hair only come up as part of the styling.

Does it work for men too?

Yes. Theo dresses anyone — suits, sneakers, and that overshirt that keeps turning up at the office.

Which devices does it work on?

iPhone, with iOS 17 or later. Theo is for ages 16 and up.

Can I cancel?

Any time, in your Apple Account subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before the free week ends and you won’t be charged.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-up, no email, no password. Open the app and show him your outfit.

What are you wearing today?

Show Theo. He’ll tell you — nicely, or not, your call.
